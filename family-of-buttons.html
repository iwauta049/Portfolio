the brief

Project overview

Sketch, design, implement, and document a web/software application in which the interaction centres around exactly one button per team member, such that:

  • Each button represents one team member’s personality or likeness in some way.
  • Interactions with the application reveal aspects of the dynamics between the team members, e.g., highlighting similarities/differences between {experiences, values, personalities} of the team members.

first sketches

Initial ideation

During the initial ideation phase, we first brainstormed our personalities as well as our hobbies to create unique button designs that reflect each team member's characteristics. We then sketched out our ideas and created sketches to visualize the button designs and their interactions. The sketches helped us to refine our ideas and select the best concepts for implementation.

Reaction sketch: if a person is a leader type, clicking on their button will burst everyone else's buttons (like a chain reaction).

choosing a direction

Design decision

After comparing initial sketches and different design concepts to express ourselves as distinct buttons, we selected the idea of representing the members as stars in the sky, and all of us being part of the same constellation. The reason for selecting this idea was that it represents a story of how we are all connected and how we can work together as a group to achieve a common goal.

1 / 2

Constellation sketch: different connections between the stars represent different relationships between the team members (similarities, differences, etc.).

the finished sky

Final product

For the final product, we used Figma to create background designs and digitally drew the interactive elements, including different star states. To showcase our team to its fullest extent, these features were implemented:

  • Clicking on a star reveals that person's characteristics and hobbies.
  • Different reaction times to hovering depending on the personality of the person: extraverts have a faster reaction time.
  • Dragging a star close to the sun/moon reveals that person's lifestyle (morning/night person).
  • Selecting two or more stars highlights shared hobbies and a descriptive caption about their personality.

Our stars not only represent our personalities, but also our relationships with each other with respect to the constellation theme.
